Activity has a new Health tab. It lists every background job with when it last ran, when it runs next, and whether its last run worked, with a Run now button for the jobs that can be started early. Below that, Connections shows whether your download clients and indexers are ready. The tab shows how many jobs are failing so a broken job is visible from anywhere in Activity. On phones, page tabs now scroll sideways instead of wrapping.
