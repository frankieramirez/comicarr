Dates, times and numbers in the web UI now follow your browser's regional format instead of always using US format. With a browser set to English (UK), for example, release days and timestamps read day-first with a 24-hour clock. This is the groundwork for translating Comicarr: when a translation is available, the account menu offers a Language picker and Comicarr opens in your browser's language.
