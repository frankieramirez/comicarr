Two story-arc features are now deprecated: downloading an arc issue whose series is not in your library straight into the arc folder, and matching files from a manual folder scan to an arc when their series is not in your library. Both still work, and neither is removed before 1.0.0. Use **Add what's missing** on the story arc instead: it adds the arc's series to your library and marks just the issues the arc needs as Wanted, so they download, import and copy to the arc folder the normal way. If your install uses either feature, Comicarr logs one warning for it at startup. Details are on the [Deprecations page](https://comicarr.com/docs/deprecations).
