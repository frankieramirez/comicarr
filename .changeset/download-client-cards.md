Settings → Download clients now shows one card for each kind of client Comicarr sends releases to: your usenet client, your torrent client and direct downloads. Each card shows the client, its server, and whether Comicarr last reached it, with a switch to stop sending that kind of release without losing its settings. Expand a card to change the client or edit its connection, then **Test connection** after saving. NZBGet, qBittorrent, Deluge and Transmission can now be set up here instead of in `config.ini`, including qBittorrent's per-library category and save-path routes. **Add client** turns on a kind you don't use yet. When you change a client's server address, Comicarr asks for its password again, so a saved password is never sent to a new server.
