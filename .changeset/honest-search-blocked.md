When Comicarr has no indexer or no download client, every screen now says so instead of showing progress that can't happen. The dashboard's Needs you lists each missing step with a button that opens the right settings page, rather than "Nothing needs you". System health reads "Search paused: no indexer" instead of "Search running". On Releases, issues that are out show "Can't search" under Needs you instead of "Searching", and upcoming ones say "search paused". Wanted shows "Search paused" instead of "Queued". Both pages show one banner with the fix. On a series page, the main button becomes "Add an indexer to search". Everything goes back to normal as soon as an indexer and a client are set up.
