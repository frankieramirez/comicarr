The issue page now shows what its last search actually saw. "What happened" says how many results came back, how many were rejected and why (wrong issue, wrong series, too small and so on), and the Details panel lists the acceptable candidates with the one Comicarr chose, the rejected ones on request, how many times each step ran, and the log lines that mention the issue with a link to the full log. When a finished download couldn't be found, **Import manually** on the issue page and in Needs you lets you point Comicarr at the folder and file so it can import it. The search history is kept per issue and replaced by each new search; it starts filling in after you upgrade.
