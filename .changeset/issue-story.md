An issue's page now tells its story. "What happened" lists every search, grab, download and import for that issue, oldest first, in plain sentences. When the issue is in Needs you, the page names the likely cause, links to the setting that usually fixes it, and puts Retry and Search again next to the title. The title now reads as the series and number, such as "Absolute Flash #18", with the issue's own name underneath.
