See what a Library count is made of without opening the series. Point at a Have count in the Library list (or tap it) and a small panel shows the breakdown: for manga, how many volumes you have, how many chapters sit inside those volumes and how many are separate chapter files, with the same coverage bar as the series page; for comics, how many issues you have and how many are wanted. Pointing at a series title shows a preview with the cover, publisher, year, status, description and latest release date.
