A Series can now be cataloged as a Magazine, alongside Comic and Manga, from the "Catalog this as" control on its page. Magazines search, download and import exactly like comics. Set a Magazine destination under Settings → Media Management → Series Folders, and cataloging a Series as Magazine moves it to its automatic folder there. Files already on disk stay where they are and are still read from their old folder. A Series whose folder you chose yourself keeps it.
