A manga volume file found by a library scan or a series rescan is now recorded as a volume, the same way a downloaded volume is. Before, a scan marked each chapter in that volume as its own download, so automatic search kept looking for the volume you already had. A volume file was also skipped completely when MangaDex lists no chapters for that volume, which happens for many long series.

After your next rescan, the chapters inside a volume you own show as Covered, and the series' Have count includes them.
