Manga series now show volumes and chapters side by side, so you can own volumes 1–20 as books and later chapters as single files.

- A new **Volumes** tab lists every volume, which chapters it holds, its file, and whether it's wanted. You can mark a volume wanted or not wanted, and edit its chapter range.
- On the **Chapters** tab, a chapter inside a volume you have reads **Covered · in vol N**, separate from a chapter you have as its own file. A new "In a volume" filter lists them, and a Vol column shows each chapter's volume.
- A coverage bar above the tabs runs from chapter 1 to the latest. It shows what you can read, what's missing before your latest chapter, and what's wanted.
- MangaDex lists the chapters of only some volumes. For long series, many volumes have no chapter list. Comicarr now estimates those ranges from the volumes around them and marks them "estimated". An owned volume covers the chapters in its estimated range, and automatic search skips them. If an estimate is off, edit the range.
- Automatic search leaves a volume you mark not wanted alone.
