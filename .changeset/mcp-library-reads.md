AI agents connected over MCP can now read more of your library: one series with its folder, issue counts and next announced issue (`get_series`), a series' issues filtered by state (`list_issues`), what is Wanted, issue by issue or grouped by series (`list_wanted`), and why an issue is not here yet, with what its last search found and rejected (`get_issue_story`). These are read-only and work with MCP writes off.
