Prowlarr search can now include your Usenet indexers. Turn on **Include Usenet indexers** under Settings → Search → Prowlarr, and NZB results come from a provider named Prowlarr (Usenet) and go to your Usenet download client, so Prowlarr counts Comicarr's searches toward each indexer's limits. Results grabbed through Prowlarr, torrent or Usenet, are now told apart correctly in your download history: before this, every Prowlarr result shared one ID.
