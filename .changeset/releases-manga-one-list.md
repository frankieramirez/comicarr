Releases now shows this week's manga, manhwa and manhua alongside comics, so you can find new series to add as well as track your own. They come from MangaUpdates, need MangaDex or MyAnimeList turned on, and fill in about a minute after you first open the page.

- Mine and Industry are now one list. Turn on **Only my library** for the old Mine view, with Needs you, In progress, Downloaded, Coming up and Search wanted.
- Format chips (All, Comics, Manga, Manhwa, Manhua) narrow the list. Manga formats also get a day strip, because they come out every day of the week.
- **Popular only** (on by default) keeps manga to MangaUpdates' weekly top 500 plus your own series. Turn it off to see every release, about 1,500 series a week.
- Every row shows its cover when one is available. **Add** on a manga row finds the series on MangaDex or MyAnimeList so you can confirm the match before it is added.
- With Only my library off, your own releases still show even when the comics pull source is down.
