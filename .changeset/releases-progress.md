Releases now shows where each of this week's downloads stands. A release that's downloading shows a progress bar and its percentage when the client is SABnzbd, NZBGet or qBittorrent. Other clients still show "Downloading" without a percentage. Once the file is with Comicarr, the row says "Importing". Downloaded releases say when they landed, for example "2h ago", "yesterday" or "Tue 21:40". When a release needs you and its cause has a one-click fix, such as **Open post-processing**, the button sits on the row. "See why" now opens the issue's own page instead of the series.
