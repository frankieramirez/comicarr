Removed four integrations that only worked through hand-edited `config.ini` settings and whose sites no longer work: the 32P torrent tracker, Experimental NZB search through NZBIndex, series data from the Grand Comics Database, and the PreviewsWorld pull-list scraper. If your install still had 32P, Experimental search or the Grand Comics Database switched on, Activity → Health now shows a row saying so. Series added from the Grand Comics Database no longer refresh; add them again from ComicVine to keep them up to date.
