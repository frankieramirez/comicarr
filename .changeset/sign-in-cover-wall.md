The sign-in page can show a wall of your own covers behind the sign-in card. Turn it on in Settings > Interface with "Cover wall on sign-in". It is off by default because anyone who can reach the sign-in page sees the covers. Series rated Mature, Adult or explicit are never shown, and the wall mixes comics and manga. The sign-in page no longer shows the Comicarr version.
