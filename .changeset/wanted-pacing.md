Wanted now shows how auto-search is pacing itself. A banner above the series list shows how many issues an hour the backlog check gets through, measured from recent runs. It also shows how long until every Wanted issue has been checked once, with a progress bar, and when the next batch runs. It explains that issues added in the last couple of weeks also get a full search on your indexers. Each queued series shows roughly when its turn comes, for example "Queued · ~2 days". If RSS is off, the banner says that only recent issues are searched automatically and links to Search settings. **Search now** moves from the page header into the banner.
