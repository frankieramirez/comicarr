The web UI is now available in Arabic, and it lays itself out right to left when an Arabic (or other right-to-left) language is active: the sidebar moves to the right, spacing and alignment mirror, menus and tooltips open toward the reading direction, and arrows point the way the text flows. Comicarr opens in Arabic when your browser's preferred language is Arabic, and you can switch languages from the Language picker in the account menu. Some messages written by the server, such as the reasons shown under Needs you, are still in English for now.
