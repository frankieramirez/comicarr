The web UI is now available in Spanish. Comicarr opens in Spanish when your browser's preferred language is Spanish, and you can switch between English and Spanish from the Language picker in the account menu. The choice is saved per browser, so people sharing one Comicarr can each use their own language. Some messages written by the server, such as the reasons shown under Needs you, are still in English for now.
