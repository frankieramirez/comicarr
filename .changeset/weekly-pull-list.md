Releases → Industry now works as a week-by-week pull list. Use the arrows to step back up to a year or ahead up to four weeks, and "This week" to return. A week Comicarr has not saved yet is fetched when you open it, from Walksoftly or, if that fails, from ComicVine. Opening a week only saves its list and never changes the status of any issue. Each release shows whether it is Wanted, Downloaded, In library, or Not in library, with a count of each above the list. You can filter by title or publisher and sort by title, publisher, or issue number. For a series you don't have, Add can add the series alone or also mark that issue Wanted once the series finishes adding. For a series you have, Want marks that issue Wanted and starts a search. Titles open the series in your library, or its ComicVine page when you don't have it.
